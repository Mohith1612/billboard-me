# Billboard.me: payment-provider and deployment research

Accessed: **2026-09-14**. Primary sources only. This is a planning note, not provider approval or a legal determination. No credentials, provider accounts, or live transactions were used. Repository versions supplied by the audit: Next.js **16.3.5**, React **19.2.8**, `postgres` **^3.4.9**, Drizzle **^0.45.2**; no deployment configuration exists. Some live provider documentation differs materially from cached search results.

## Decision summary

**Payment eligibility is an early product gate, not the final integration chore.** Razorpay Route's live documentation includes a turnover condition that may exclude Billboard.me at its present stage. Cashfree Easy Split is a credible functional alternative, but its public pages do not establish that a prerevenue marketplace qualifies. Stripe India has material availability and funds-flow limitations. Dodo's policy excludes the proposed marketplace model. Choose **one** provider only after written confirmation of the actual legal entity, individual-seller eligibility, physical sponsorship category, fees, liability, and permissible settlement timing.

Do not promise “payout after proof” until the approved provider flow can enforce it for the chosen campaign duration. Customer authorization, captured payment, an allocation/transfer to a seller's provider account, and successful bank settlement are distinct events. App records should report them separately.

### Razorpay: consequential details

The Route FAQ confirms both the turnover and payer-payee requirements. A self-declaration may replace GST returns for some businesses; it is **not described as a waiver of the turnover threshold**. The FAQ says access cannot continue below the threshold. It describes a possible two-step alternative: money settles to the merchant, then the merchant pays vendors. That description is not approval for Billboard.me to hold client funds or operate its own escrow; changing to an agency or seller-direct commercial model requires separate founder, provider and professional review. [Route compliance FAQ](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/route/faqs.md).

This is supported by primary regulation: the RBI's 2025 PA Directions, section 16, Table 1, permitted debits (iv), attach turnover and payer-payee conditions to the described third-party debit path. Section 13 also discusses merchant due diligence and onboarding marketplace sellers. The legal classification of Billboard.me's arrangement needs review; **do not infer that every alternative commercial model is forbidden or automatically permitted**. [RBI PA Directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12896&Mode=0). Razorpay's own terms also contain the turnover representation in section 14.2. [Razorpay terms](https://razorpay.com/terms/).

Linked Account setup collects business/KYC and bank information. Documentation describes penny testing and blocks transfers until bank validation succeeds. The linked-account page's settlement timing text differs from the configurable schedule page; the contracted configuration must resolve that discrepancy. Store provider onboarding/capability states and references, and avoid unnecessarily retaining raw identity or bank documents in application storage. [Linked Accounts](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/route/linked-account.md).

The schedule documentation supports `on_hold` and `on_hold_until` semantics, including an indefinite hold description. **That API behavior is not evidence of unrestricted legal/commercial holding permission.** Obtain an approved maximum duration, exceptions handling and deadline before choosing the booking horizon and proof-review SLA. [Schedule settlements](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/route/schedule-settlement.md).

Refunds originate from the primary account and require corresponding transfer reversal, either explicitly or with the provider's reversal option. A refund record and a transfer-reversal record must therefore be separate. [Route refunds](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/route/refund/). Route documents transfer processed/failed and settlement events; a processed transfer is not proof of seller bank receipt. Reconcile by transfer, settlement and bank reference rather than counting the first successful API response as a payout. [Route webhook events](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/route/).

Razorpay's advertised transfer idempotency header applies **only to its Direct Transfers API** on the cited page. Do not assume payment-linked transfers, checkout creation or reversals inherit that guarantee. Preserve a local operation key, unique provider references and an ambiguous-result reconciliation path for each money-moving operation. [Direct-transfer idempotency](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/payments/route/direct-transfers-idempotent-request/?preferred-country=US). Webhooks use at-least-once delivery and may duplicate; event IDs support deduplication. [Webhook FAQ](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/faqs/?preferred-country=SG).

Payment authorization must be captured within a provider-defined short window; Razorpay currently documents three days before automatic refund. Therefore leaving a payment authorized for weeks until an event is not a campaign settlement strategy. [Capture settings](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payments/capture-settings/?preferred-country=US).

### Cashfree: consequential details

Vendor setup accepts Individual or Business Entity, bank account or UPI VPA, and KYC documents. The newer vendor flow distinguishes bank-validation failure, beneficiary-creation failure, KYC review, action required, active, on hold, blocked and deleted. Documents and thresholds differ between overview pages, so implement against the approved current onboarding contract rather than copying a general table. [Add vendor](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/split/vendor/add), [Vendor KYC](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/split/vendor/kyc).

Cashfree distinguishes **time allowed to submit a split after payment** from **the vendor's settlement eligibility date**. Missing the split interval can send the full amount to the platform. A vendor settlement delay has a maximum eligibility date and is not freely extendable after that window; the published 15-day scenario is an example, not a universal allowed maximum. Confirm the account's real limits and whether release can occur only after review. [Delay overview](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/split/settlements/delay/overview), [Order split delay](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/split/settlements/delay/order-level), [Vendor defer settlement](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/split/settlements/delay/vendor-level).

Refund treatment depends on whether the split/settlement occurred; post-settlement refunds can debit vendor balances against later settlements. For a seller who never transacts again, future-offset recovery is not a reliable commercial guarantee. Require a documented liability/recovery policy and sufficient approved refund funding. [Easy Split FAQ](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/help/easy-split/faqs/faqs).

Settlement webhooks include initiated, success, failed and reversed, with a reason and bank reference. Keep failure and reversal visible to the operator and seller. [Settlement webhooks](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/payments/split/webhooks). Payment webhooks are signed using the raw request body; current documented versions can supply an idempotency key. Verify the actual API/event version, use unique payment IDs, and test multiple attempts for one order. [Payment webhooks](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/payments/webhooks). Mutation idempotency must be checked endpoint-by-endpoint during the provider spike; it was not established here for every Easy Split endpoint.

### Stripe: consequential details

India Connect onboarding requirements depend on account/business type and capability; Stripe-hosted onboarding collects verification information, while custom onboarding is restricted to certain use cases. This favors provider-managed verification if Stripe approves the model. [India Connect onboarding](https://support.stripe.com/questions/onboarding-requirements-for-stripe-connect-in-india?locale=en-GB).

The general payout page says India uses automatic daily payouts, while the India-specific marketplace support page allows manual payouts in limited cases. Treat proof-dependent release as **explicitly unverified**, not impossible for all accounts and not generally available. [Payout schedules](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts?locale=en-GB), [India marketplace restrictions](https://support.stripe.com/questions/stripe-india-support-for-marketplaces?locale=en-GB).

Outside those restrictions, separate charges/transfers can retain fees by transferring less, but charge refunds do not automatically reverse associated transfers; the platform bears specified refunds and chargebacks, and reversals depend on available connected-account balance/reserves. This illustrates why an internal `PaymentService` cannot erase commercial differences. This charge model is **not currently supported for India** per the country-specific page. [Separate charges and transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers?locale=en-GB).

Stripe supports API idempotency keys and signed webhooks with duplicate/out-of-order delivery considerations. These are useful baseline engineering properties, not a reason to select an ineligible provider. [Idempotent requests](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests), [Webhooks](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks).

## Required provider/commercial gate before payment implementation

Founder obtains a written decision covering:

1. The actual operating entity, eligibility/turnover evidence, individual-seller onboarding and sponsorship category/MCC.
2. Who contracts with the buyer, who supplies the advertising service and who issues each invoice; applicable tax/withholding responsibilities require professional confirmation.
3. An exact funds-flow diagram: payer, collection account, seller allocation, seller bank settlement; amount that the platform can retain and permitted settlement limits.
4. Campaign duration, delivery/proof/review deadlines and whether disputed work can remain unsettled within those limits. Design shorter near-term pilots if needed.
5. Fee quote covering payment processing, splits, settlements, refunds, taxes and unrecoverable dispute losses. The ₹10,000 / 15% example is a gross commercial split; ₹1,500 is not automatically net platform revenue.
6. Full/partial refund and transfer-reversal responsibility before and after payout; chargeback evidence deadlines and funding; failed/returned settlement recovery.
7. A sandbox demonstration of onboarding → payment → correctly held allocation → approved release → confirmed settlement, plus failure/retry/refund cases. Production readiness also requires contracted approval; sandbox success alone is insufficient.

If no provider approves the desired model, do not build around a fictional escrow. Founder chooses either a provider-approved simpler commercial model (for example, seller-direct collection with separately invoiced platform service), or delays paid launch. Such a change needs an ADR and revised product promises. Direct seller payment before proof does not satisfy a promised platform-controlled proof-before-payout flow.

## Minimal payment architecture recommendation

One monolith, one active provider, a small provider adapter with business-facing operations and explicit capability limits. Retain provider IDs and operation outcomes in Postgres; use a durable webhook inbox and constrained order/payment transitions. Keep payment attempt, refund, allocation/transfer and payout/settlement records separate. Repeated events and delayed captures must not create another campaign or sell the same spot twice. Reconcile unknown provider outcomes before retrying operations lacking provider idempotency. This is accounting/reconciliation of provider money movement, **not a wallet or customer spendable balance**.

Allow founder-operated proof reviews, dispute decisions and release approval for the first campaigns. Manual operation still needs timestamped actor/reason/evidence records and independently verified settlement references. A customer redirect, uploaded transfer screenshot or operator “paid” toggle is insufficient payment evidence.

## Deployment verification

OpenNext's own support page says **all Next.js 16 minor and patch releases are supported**, which nominally includes 16.3.5. It uses Next's Node runtime on Cloudflare Workers, not a full ordinary Node process, and flags unsupported Node middleware. This is a compatibility claim, **not verification that the current repository builds and runs correctly**. [OpenNext support](https://opennext.js.org/cloudflare).

Cloudflare's current guide, updated August 25, 2026, now recommends **vinext**, an API reimplementation on Vite, and explicitly labels it beta. Its OpenNext adapter documentation remains available. This is a meaningful framework/runtime choice requiring evaluation; it is not a reason to automatically migrate a first-transaction app. [Current Cloudflare Next.js guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/framework-guides/web-apps/nextjs/), [OpenNext adapter configuration](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/framework-guides/web-apps/opennext/).

| Concern | Verified fact and implication |
| --- | --- |
| Postgres driver | Cloudflare supports Postgres.js with Hyperdrive (minimum 3.4.5), so the repository's ^3.4.9 is above that minimum; `pg` is currently recommended by Cloudflare but this alone does not justify replacing the existing driver. Validate the chosen driver under actual concurrent requests. [Postgres.js on Hyperdrive](https://developers.cloudflare.com/hyperdrive/examples/connect-to-postgres/postgres-drivers-and-libraries/postgres-js/). |
| Connection lifetime | OpenNext warns against sharing database clients with request-bound I/O across Workers requests. Use the selected platform's recommended request-scoped lifecycle and pooling rather than assuming the existing module-level client is portable. [OpenNext database guide](https://opennext.js.org/cloudflare/howtos/db). |
| Better Auth | Better Auth documents Drizzle/Postgres adapters and ORM schema generation. Pin compatible packages and generate/review auth schema through Drizzle migrations; do not run Better Auth's built-in migration machinery with the Drizzle adapter. No exact-repo Workers auth behavior was tested here. [Drizzle adapter](https://better-auth.com/docs/adapters/drizzle), [Database/migrations](https://better-auth.com/docs/concepts/database). |
| Sharp | Sharp's standard build requires a Node-API-compatible runtime. Its optional WASM package requires multithreading and does not support single-threaded environments; this is not a generic Workers portability promise. Don't base Workers media processing on untested native Sharp. [Sharp installation](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/install/). |
| Images/storage | OpenNext's documented image optimization uses Cloudflare Images bindings or a custom loader, with compatibility/cost considerations. Canonical SVG templates do not need runtime raster processing. Private proof originals require access controls independently of public transformed marketing images. [OpenNext image optimization](https://opennext.js.org/cloudflare/howtos/image). |

**Recommended deployment gate:** a timeboxed spike proves the exact dependency versions, build, server route, real nonproduction DB connection under repeated/concurrent requests, planned auth session flow, private upload/read, raw-body webhook verification, and operator-triggered/scheduled reconciliation. If Cloudflare adds unresolved adapter/media complexity, select a conventional managed Node deployment for the first transaction and retain Supabase Postgres and portable object storage. This is an engineering recommendation based on integration surface, not a benchmark result. Do not replace Next.js, add Hyperdrive, or introduce another storage provider in this documentation task.

## Evidence limits

- No commercial rates were assumed, no providers contacted, and no transaction/auth/upload/deployment spike executed.
- Razorpay and RBI pages failed in the browser extraction tool but their **same official public URLs** were fetched successfully with Python `urllib`; relevant live Markdown and page text were read. Their live eligibility language supersedes missing details in cached search previews.
- Some provider documentation has inconsistent examples or dates (Route FY references/schedules; Cashfree KYC/delay examples; generic versus India-specific Stripe payout text). These are explicit questions for the implementation spike and provider agreement, not gaps to fill from memory.
