## Reading an issue

Each body includes Problem, Context, Dependencies, Scope, Out of scope, Proposed implementation, Files/components likely affected, Acceptance criteria, Tests required, Documentation required, Risks and Notes. The exact next step is in Notes. Read the issue before touching files; do not infer requirements from its short title.

Foundation work does not wait for commercial demand to protect existing leads. Payment implementation must wait for PAY-001 approval. DOMAIN-001 and DECISION-001 record founder decisions; agents cannot approve their own architecture or legal/commercial policies. The full P0 sequence is the first complete vertical slice, with manual founder fulfillment and no public catalogue.
