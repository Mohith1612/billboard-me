# First-campaign operating runbook

**Proposed; no completed campaign is established here.** Founder owns marketplace curation/support and authorized live platform actions. Seller owns physical fulfillment; sponsor evaluates and supplies agreed creative/approvals. [Lifecycle](../architecture/transaction-lifecycle.md) owns transition authority; [PRD](../product/prd.md) owns policy. OPS-001 tools do not exist yet.

## Before offering a payable placement

Record the seller identity/context and specific rights/fit checks, dates and check scope. Seller attests to control, team/event/employer permission and sponsor conflicts. Trial activated template dimensions, application/removal, costs and lead time. A curated listing is not a promise of future attendance or advertising effectiveness.

Agree the sponsor’s budget authority, spot/period/deliverables, named production owner (normally seller), creative/cost obligations, evidence requirement/formats and total/estimated seller share. Explain normal provider settlement and support/cancellation/refund terms. Existing marketing fee/print/proof-release promises need the approved correction/transition before new paid agreements.

PAY-001 requires written approval of entity/category, individual sellers, fee allocation and ordinary settlement, KYC, refunds/reversals/disputes and actual loss responsibility. DECISION-001 approves party/platform obligations, fee/waiver, taxes/invoices, policy deadlines, evidence/retention and support authority. **Requires external validation:** applicable provider/legal/advertising/tax obligations. Do not assume that removing holds permits an otherwise ineligible funds flow.

Evidence deadlines do not need to fit inside settlement timing. Verify any provider advance-booking/risk restrictions independently. P0 can use private text/HTTPS evidence references and attributed sponsor responses; if retained original files are required, approve a secure retention process or advance MEDIA-001 before accepting the order. Never promise durable file storage that does not exist.

## Release gate

LAUNCH-001 rehearses payment and normal settlement **without proof approval**, seller fulfillment/evidence/sponsor response, nonperformance after settlement and relevant failures. Verify deployed auth/private records/webhooks, migration/backup/restore, operator protection, mail visibility and reconciliation. Native-file checks apply only if enabled/required. Founder names support/escalation ownership, response window and maximum exposure; explicitly authorizes live mode after commercial/technical gates. Agents do not enable it because tests pass.

## Per-campaign record

The rows below are record requirements, not a mandated settlement-after-execution sequence.

| Track | Record / check | Stop or escalate if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualify/publish | Seller/page/listing, rights/context check provenance, actual variant/dates, accurate public claims | Rights, fit or truthful terms unclear |
| Agree | Current offer, both acceptances, frozen responsibility/evidence/settlement terms, exclusive reservation | Stale terms, unwanted creative or inventory conflict |
| Collect | Provider-confirmed capture/account/amount/currency and active seller capability | Unknown, late or mismatched capture; no execution based on browser return |
| Allocate/settle | Actual fee/share breakdown, provider allocation and bank reference/status per order | Unknown/failed/reversed settlement, KYC/risk restriction, unreconciled totals; no proof gate |
| Prepare/execute | Seller reports, sponsor creative approval, actual dates/context, material-change messages | Seller nonattendance, missed lead time, cancelled event or prohibited placement; notify parties |
| Evidence/respond | Agreed requirement, private notes/references, submit/capture dates, sponsor acknowledgement/clarification/objection | Missing required evidence, disappearing links, disagreement or sponsor silence; no implicit consent |
| Support/remedy | Both accounts, accepted terms, support authority/party agreement, financial provider records | Unresolved facts, recovery shortage or chargeback deadline; no guaranteed outcome |
| Close/learn | Completion basis, attributed evidence, independently reconciled settlement, platform support cost and party feedback | Unconfirmed performance, unresolved complaint or financial reversal cannot be called verified success |

No direct mark-paid database edits. A provider dashboard action requires authorized actor/time/reason/reference and subsequent reconciliation. No raw KYC/bank/contract details in public GitHub. Public history/proof requires consent.

## Support boundary and daily checks

Review unanswered offers, expiring inventory holds with unresolved capture, funding/settlement mismatches, seller change notices, overdue agreed evidence/sponsor response, provider dispute deadlines and failed notifications. Run reconciliation. Failed email or sponsor silence never creates consent, verified fulfillment or a product settlement hold.

For seller/sponsor disagreement, retain both accounts and frozen terms/messages/evidence; seek party resolution and apply approved policy where authorized. Allow an explicit unresolved/escalated outcome. Founder support does not have to certify every physical fact. A provider/network decides its financial dispute; statutory remedies and actual contract/provider liability are not overridden by product language.

Nonattendance after settlement is a seller fulfillment failure with support/refund/dispute consequences under applicable terms. Do not promise that settled funds can always be recovered. The platform still owns remedies for its own technical/policy failures. Separate buyer refund, seller recovery and settlement reversal outcomes; record unrecovered exposure.

Pause affected **new** listing/checkout activity when warranted, not by pretending an already-settled payment is frozen. Query ambiguous provider operations before retry. Reschedules require both parties and fresh rights/availability checks. Settlement failure does not erase completed physical work.

## Learning checkpoint

The north star is a conjunction: captured payment, sponsor-acknowledged evidence-backed completion and successful provider bank settlement. These may occur in different sequences. Report evidence-free/unconfirmed completions separately, exclude tests/self-funded demos and adjust current qualified counts for disputes/reversals without deleting history. Founder confirms the measurement rule in DECISION-001.

After 1–5 campaigns, compare actual platform fees, support minutes, losses and repeat intent against seller/party production costs. Decide continue, narrow or pause. Repeat at ten and each expansion toward one hundred; volume is not an automatic public-marketplace gate.
