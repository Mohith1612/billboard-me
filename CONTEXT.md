# Billboard.me language

This glossary defines the proposed product model. [PRD](docs/product/prd.md) owns requirements; [current state](docs/status/current-state.md) records what exists.

## People and inventory

**Seller:** A person approved to sell sponsorship on an object they own or have permission to use. A social following is one possible distribution signal, not the definition of a seller.

**Buyer:** The person agreeing to and purchasing sponsorship, including a person acting for a brand.

**Operator:** The founder or an explicitly authorized person carrying out approval, fulfillment and support work.

**Asset:** A particular physical object controlled by a seller, such as their running jersey.

**Asset template:** A reusable, versioned specification for an asset type, its appearance and permitted placements.

**Surface:** A physical side or region of a template, such as a garment's front, back or sleeve.

**Spot definition:** A predefined placement within a surface, such as the chest sponsorship rectangle.

**Inventory spot:** The realization of a spot definition on one particular asset. This is the thing that cannot be sold to competing buyers for overlapping periods.

**Distribution signal:** A contextual claim about who may encounter the seller or asset, including audience, physical visibility, frequency, place and event. It is not a guaranteed impression count.

**Sponsorship page:** A shareable presentation of a seller's sponsorship opportunity in a stated context, grouping one or more listings.
_Avoid_: Campaign (before an order is funded).

**Listing:** An offer to sell one inventory spot for a defined period, price and deliverables.

## Transactions and delivery

**Offer:** A buyer's or seller's proposed terms for a listing, including an asking-price request. A counteroffer is a new revision of those terms.

**Reservation:** Temporary or confirmed exclusive allocation of a spot for a period while an order is paid or fulfilled.

**Order:** The mutually accepted commercial agreement, including the purchased placement and frozen terms.

**Payment:** Money collected from the buyer through the approved provider.

**Campaign:** The execution of the sponsorship purchased in a funded order.

**Deliverable:** An explicit seller or operator obligation that the buyer agreed to purchase.

**Proof:** Submitted evidence intended to show that agreed campaign deliverables were fulfilled.

**Transfer:** A provider's allocation or movement of collected funds to a seller's linked provider account. It does not by itself establish bank receipt.

**Payout:** Settlement of the seller's proceeds to their bank through the approved provider.

**Refund:** Money returned to the buyer through the provider.

**Reversal:** A provider's reversal of a prior transfer or settlement; distinct from a buyer refund.

**Dispute:** A claim that a commercial obligation or payment is contested, raised through support or the payment provider.

**Completed paid campaign:** A fulfilled campaign with approved proof and successful seller payout, subject to the reporting rules in the PRD.
